# ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

This artifact and the surrounding "Mothership" architectural framework are dedicated to **Skrillex** (Sonny Moore).

## Dedication

In recognition of the **Mothership Tour (2010-2014)**—a definitive era of creative transport, technical ambition, and visual spectacle. This project draws its aesthetic soul and naming convention from that lineage: the spaceship DJ booth, the stealth bomber geometry, and the relentless pursuit of "breaking the line" between performance and technology.

## Core Inspiration

- **The Vessel**: The Mothership as a metaphor for a creative vessel carrying musical and digital ideas across the "grid."
- **Red Rocks Aesthetic**: Homage to the 2011 and 2014 Mothership shows at Red Rocks Amphitheatre—the primary color spectrum (Red Bull palette) and the industrial-organic synthesis.
- **Technical Ambition**: Mirroring the leap from simple projection to complex motion-capture and real-time visual feedback systems.
